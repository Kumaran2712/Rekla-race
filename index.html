How to play
  • ஹை! on the bell. A shout on the hoofbeat is a Perfect: more speed, less stamina. The ring on the button shows the beat.
  • Brace. Potholes, the goat and rival bumps can throw you. When BRACE flashes, tap anything.
  • Thrown off? Tap ◀ ▶ in turn to run after your cart, then ஹை! to jump back on. The cart must finish with you on it.
  • Bump. In pack races, steer into a cart beside you to slam wheels. Pushing someone into the barricade is a foul.
  • Do or Die. The last stretch: shouts hit harder but cost more.
  • Camera. The camera button or C cycles five views.
